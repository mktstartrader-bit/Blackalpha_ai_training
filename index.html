AI is reshaping the world, and Black Alpha AI Academy equips you with the knowledge, skills and confidence to make the most of it.

Frequently asked questions

        

        

          

            What is an AI academy?
            
An AI academy is a structured learning environment focused on building practical understanding of artificial intelligence. At Black Alpha, the Academy connects AI literacy, applied skills and leadership learning across different audiences, while the wider ecosystem also supports organisations with AI transformation. The aim is to help each visitor choose a relevant starting point.

          

          

            Who is the Black Alpha AI Academy for?
            
Black Alpha is designed for four main audiences: young learners aged 10–18, working professionals, senior leaders and organisations. Each group has a different route, from creative AI projects and workplace skills to executive education and enterprise transformation. Visitors can start with the academy that matches their current role and goal.

          

          

            How do I start learning AI?
            
Start with the outcome you want. Young learners can begin with age-based creator, builder or founder programs. Professionals can focus on productivity before progressing to building tools and applying AI across business functions. Executives can explore strategy and governance, while organisations can begin with an AI readiness assessment and roadmap.

          

          

            What is AI training?
            
AI training develops the knowledge and practical skills needed to use artificial intelligence effectively in a specific context. At Black Alpha, the focus is project-led and role-based: learners may work on research, writing, data, automation, AI tools, strategy or governance depending on the academy and level they choose.

          

          

            Are the programs online?
            
Yes. Most Black Alpha programs are designed for online delivery, with live instruction and practical activities. This means participants can learn AI online while still working through guided projects and exercises. Customised in-person options may also be available for selected programs, private cohorts and organisations, depending on the learning format required.

          

          

            Do I need a technical background to join?
            
Not necessarily. The Academy is built around different starting points, from young learners and working professionals to executives. Some pathways focus on practical use, productivity, strategy or governance rather than advanced coding. Any specific prerequisites should be stated on the individual academy page so participants can choose the level that matches their experience.

          

          

            Can my company or team take part together?
            
Yes. Black Alpha offers enterprise and in-house formats alongside individual learning pathways. Teams can explore industry-specific training, executive programs, AI readiness work, strategy workshops and customised delivery. The appropriate format depends on the organisation’s objectives, team profile and stage of AI adoption, so the next step is to discuss the requirement with an advisor.

          

          

            Which academy should I start with?
            
Choose the academy that matches your current role. Ages 10–18 start with AI Native Academy. Working adults start with AI Professional Academy. C-suite and senior leaders start with Executive Academy. Organisations looking at readiness, strategy or implementation start with Enterprise AI Transformation. You can enter at the stage that fits you without completing the other pathways first.
